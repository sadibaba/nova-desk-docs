## 🔴 Sabse Important Discovery: Tumhare Account Lockout Ne Cascade Kar Diya

Dekho pattern:
```
Section 3 (rate limit test): 401, 401, 401, 401, 401, 403, 403, 403... (5 ke baad 403 shuru)
Section 5 (token reuse):     auth/me → 403, storage → 403, search → 403, portfolio → OK
```

**Yahi hai asal kahani:** Tumhara system **account lockout** implement karta hai (5 galat attempts ke baad), jo achi baat hai (security feature kaam kar raha hai!). Lekin Section 3 ne jaan-bujh kar `testEmail` (baseline user) ko **20 galat password attempts** se lock kar diya. Us lockout ka asar tha ke baadwale calls bhi 403 dene lage — lekin **portfolio successful raha** matlab portfolio route lockout check nahi kar raha (alag middleware use kar raha hoga), jabke `auth/me`, `storage`, `search` shayad ek common middleware use karte hain jo locked account ko globally block kar raha hai.

**Ye genuinely accha hai documentation ke liye:** "Account lockout mechanism confirmed working — 5 failed attempts triggers block (403), though it currently returns 403 instead of standard 429, and worth verifying it doesn't over-block unrelated legitimate requests."

### Health Check 401 Kyun Fail Hua?
Ye thoda alarming hai — health check ko **kabhi bhi auth ki zaroorat nahi honi chahiye**. Ho sakta hai:
- Tumhare pehle wale heavy k6 stress tests (1000-2000 VUs) ne IP-level rate limiter already trigger kar diya ho jo abhi tak active hai
- Ya health route accidentally kisi global auth middleware ke peeche aa gaya ho

**Test:** Thodi der (5-10 min) wait karo (agar lockout time-based hai), phir sirf health check akela try karo. Agar phir bhi 401 aaye, to health route ko `app.js` mein dekho — kahin `authenticate` middleware accidentally pehle apply to nahi ho raha sabhi routes pe.

Verify OTP: 3842 ms ← bohot slow
Login: 89 ms         ← ye fast hai

Interesting: **Login fast hai (89ms)** lekin **Register/Verify bohot slow hain** — bina kisi concurrent load ke bhi. Ye batata hai bcrypt rounds ka masla nahi tha (warna login bhi slow hota), balke **register/verify-otp ke andar koi specific slow operation hai** — jaise email bhejna (agar `await` laga ke synchronously wait kar rahe ho email service ka), ya OTP generate/hash karne mein extra slow step.

**Ye dhoondo:** `register` aur `verify-otp` controller dekho — kya wahan koi `await sendEmail(...)` jaisi cheez hai jo response se pehle complete hone ka wait kar rahi hai? Agar haan, **yahi tumhara 6 second wala bottleneck hai** (jaisa pehle analysis document mein bhi suggest tha — "email async queue mein daalo").
