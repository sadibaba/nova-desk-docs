## Teen Options — Konsa Tumhare Liye Sahi Hai

| Structure | Kab Sahi Hai | Liability Protection |
|---|---|---|
| **Sole Proprietorship** | Sabse simple, SECP registration ki zaroorat hi nahi | ❌ Nahi — tumhari personal property risk pe hoti hai agar business pe case/loan ho |
| **SMC (Single Member Company)** | Tum akele founder ho, lekin corporate status + protection chahiye | ✅ Haan — company alag legal entity hai |
| **Private Limited Company** | Kam se kam 2 directors/shareholders chahiye (co-founder ho to) | ✅ Haan |

Chunki tum akele Nova Desk bana rahe ho, **SMC (Single Member Company)** tumhare liye sabse natural fit lagta hai — sirf ek hi banda director + shareholder ho sakta hai, aur company ban jati hai, jo companies ke sath deal karne (B2B clients, jo tumhare business model ka hissa hai) ke liye credibility deti hai.

## Process — Kaise Hota Hai (2026 Ke Mutabiq)

1. **SECP ke LEAP eZfile portal** pe account banao (CNIC/mobile/email chahiye)
2. **Name reservation** — 3 proposed names submit karo (Nova Desk available hai ya nahi, pehle check karo)
3. **Incorporation application** — MOA/AOA (eZfile khud generate kar deta hai), Form-1/21/29
4. **Fee pay karo** — authorized capital ke hisaab se (chhoti capital, ~PKR 100,000 tak, ke liye fee **kaafi kam hai**, different sources PKR 1,800-2,500 range bata rahe hain — exact figure eZfile portal pe hi confirm hoga, sources thoda vary kar rahe hain)
5. **1-3 working days** mein incorporation certificate mil jata hai (digital)

**Post-registration (zaroori, bhoolna mat):**
- **FBR se NTN** (tax number) lena — 7 din ke andar
- **GIS + beneficial ownership filing** — SECP ko 30 din ke andar
- **Trademark check/registration** — yeh **SECP se alag** hai, **IPO Pakistan** ke through hota hai. Zaroori hai — SECP pe naam available hone ka matlab yeh nahi ke koi aur pehle se trademark kar chuka. Pehle IPO Pakistan ki database pe "Nova Desk" search karo.

## Ek Zaroori Sochne Wali Baat (Timing)

Yeh purely tumhara decision hai, lekin factually yeh cheezein consider karna:

**Abhi register karne ke fayde:**
- Naam protect ho jata hai
- Companies (jo tumhara paid-tier target hai) registered entity ke sath deal karna zyada comfortable feel karti hain
- Business bank account khul sakta hai
- Personal asset protection mil jati hai (SMC ke through)

**Abhi register karne ka trade-off:**
- Registration ke baad **recurring compliance obligations** shuru ho jati hain (annual return, GIS filing) — **chahe revenue ho ya na ho**, deadlines miss karne pe penalty lagti hai
- Product abhi active development mein hai (backend bhi complete nahi hua) — kuch log advice dete hain pehle MVP/paying customer confirm karo, phir incorporate karo, taake compliance ka burden tab aaye jab business genuinely chal rahi ho

**Koi bhi "sahi" jawab nahi hai** — depend karta hai tumhare risk-tolerance aur timeline pe. Agar jaldi kisi client/investor se baat karni hai, abhi register karna sahi hoga. Agar sirf apne liye build kar rahe ho aur launch mein waqt hai, thoda wait kar ke bhi kar sakte ho — company hamesha baad mein bhi ban sakti hai.
